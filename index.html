🪪 Lamination & Printed Signs

Choose whether you need lamination only or a complete printed and laminated sign.

Standard — 125 microns
Flexible and durable protection for regular documents and everyday use.

Heavy-Duty — 250 microns
Thicker and more rigid protection for items that need extra durability.

💡 Micron (µ) refers to the thickness of the laminating film. Higher micron means thicker and more rigid protection.
🪪 ID Lamination

ID-size lamination currently uses our pre-cut Standard 125-micron pouch for a clean and consistent finish.
